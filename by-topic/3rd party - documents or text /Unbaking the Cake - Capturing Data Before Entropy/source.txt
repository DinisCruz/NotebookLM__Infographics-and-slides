We are spending billions trying to unbake the cake.

The entire Generative AI industry is currently obsessed with one problem: "How do we make sense of the massive volume of unstructured data?"

We are building Vector Databases, RAG pipelines, and massive ingestion engines to read PDFs, slide decks, and reports. We are treating "Unstructured Data" as a natural resource to be mined.

But here is the uncomfortable truth: There is no such thing as naturally occurring unstructured enterprise data.

That 50-page PDF report? It started as a row in a database. That bridge inspection summary? It started as a measurement on a sensor. That clinical trial output? It started as a form entry.

Data is born structured. It only becomes "unstructured" because we force-compress it into documents so humans can read it. In the process, we strip away lineage, metadata, and semantic context. We introduce Data Entropy.

💲 Then, we turn around and pay LLM companies millions of dollars to "guess" the structure we just destroyed.

We are breaking the data, and then buying expensive glue to fix it.

💥 The future of Enterprise AI isn't better RAG. It’s better, Capture.
🛑 We need to stop trying to reverse-engineer the soup. We need to capture the data at the source in its native, semantic state, before we flatten it into a document.

If you capture the meaning at the point of creation, you don't need to hallucinate the context later.

Stop fixing unstructured data. Start preventing it.
